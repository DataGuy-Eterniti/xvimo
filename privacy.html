Privacy

Xvimo public beta · last updated 8 October 2026

What Xvimo does with your message

When you check a message or screenshot, it is sent to AI models (NVIDIA Nemotron, and MiniCPM-V for screenshots) running on Nebius Token Factory, and the agent may search the web with Tavily for related public information. Your message is used only to produce your result.

What we keep

  • A short preview of the message (first 160 characters) with long numbers and email addresses masked, the verdict, and timing.
  • For WhatsApp, only the last four digits of your number. The web demo stores no name, number or account.
  • Screenshots are not stored — only the text read from them, masked as above.
  • Your 👍 / 👎 rating, if you give one.
  • To count how many people use Xvimo, your browser keeps a random ID (not linked to your name, number or device), and we note which link brought you here (for example LinkedIn). For WhatsApp we keep a one-way scrambled code of your number, which cannot be turned back into it. No sign-in, no advertising cookies.

We use this to measure how well Xvimo works and to improve it. We do not sell or share it. During the beta it is kept in our secure cloud database (Neon); you can ask us to delete it at any time.

Please don't paste

Passwords, PINs, OTP codes, BVN, card or account numbers. Xvimo never needs them — and anyone asking for them is a red flag.

Limits

Xvimo gives guidance, not a guarantee. AI can be wrong. When money is involved, confirm through official channels.

Your rights

Under Nigeria's Data Protection Act 2023 you can ask what we hold about you or ask us to delete it. Contact: privacy@facetrust.ai
